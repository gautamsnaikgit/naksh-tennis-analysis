## Research Incorporated

### Elite Player Development at Age 8
- **Djokovic:** Discovered by Jelena Genčić at age 6. She coached him for 6 years with iron discipline. He trained at Partizan Tennis Club under Bogdan Obradović.
- **Federer:** Multi-sport athlete until age 12. Started regional group at 8, added weekly private coaching (Adolf Kacovsky) at 10, then Peter Carter for technique.
- **Nadal:** Toni Nadal (uncle) coached 1:1 from age 3. Intense focus on footwork (box drill), consistency, and mental toughness. Never left Mallorca.
- **Alcaraz:** Trained at father's academy. "Natural tennis" and net approaches at age 8. Visual sharpness and reaction speed drills. Joined Equelite (Juan Carlos Ferrero) at 15.
- **Coco Gauff:** Father + Sly Black coached technique. Movement specialist (Jeff Drock) added at age 8 for cone drills. Deliberately limited tournament play to focus on fed-ball drills.

### USTA Guidelines for Age 8
- Maximum 4 tennis sessions per week
- 10-12 hours total physical activity/week (including other sports)
- Recommended structure: 3 group + 1 private session
- Max 1 tournament weekend per month
- Orange ball → Green ball transition period

### The 4 Pillars (USTA/ITF)
1. **Technical:** Grips, swing paths, contact points, footwork
2. **Tactical:** Court positioning, shot selection, pattern play
3. **Physical:** Agility, Balance, Coordination, Speed (ABCs)
4. **Mental:** Focus, emotional control, resilience, love for the game

### Private vs Group Lessons Research
- **Private/Semi-Private:** Best for technique correction, personalized feedback, building fundamentals
- **Group:** Best for match-play simulation, peer motivation, tactical adaptability, affordable reps
- **Industry Standard:** Hybrid model — group base + targeted private supplement
- Every elite player had a dedicated technical mentor (1:1 or 1:2) by age 8
